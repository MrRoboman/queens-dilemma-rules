# House Rulings

Rulings our group has agreed on, either for questions the rulebook doesn't clearly answer or as deliberate changes to a rule. **For our campaign, these win over the rulebook and over card text.**

## How to add a ruling

Add a new entry at the bottom, numbered in order, in this format:

```markdown
### H1. Short question being settled
- **Ruling:** What we decided, in one or two sentences.
- **Why:** What the rulebook says or doesn't say, with a page link, e.g. [p. 24](https://mrroboman.github.io/queens-dilemma-rules/?page=24). Say "House rule" if we're deliberately changing a printed rule.
- **Agreed:** Game N, YYYY-MM-DD
```

If we later change our minds, edit the entry and add the new date rather than deleting it.
