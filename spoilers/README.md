# ⚠️ Spoilers: Campaign Rules

**If you aren't playing in our campaign, stop here.** This folder records the content our group has revealed while playing The Queen's Dilemma, including cards, Mystery box rule sheets and story outcomes.

## What's here

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`CAMPAIGN_RULES.md`](CAMPAIGN_RULES.md) | Everything revealed so far that affects the rules, transcribed word for word. AI agents read this file along with `RULES.md` when answering rules questions. |
| `photos/` | Reference photos of the revealed components, named by code (e.g. `C012.jpg`). |

## What to record

Record anything that has been **revealed to the whole table** and affects how the game is played:

- Mystery box rule sheets
- Event cards
- Chronicle cards, including who is the Herald
- Skill stickers, with their owner
- Building stickers, with their Region
- Narrative Keyword stickers, with their owner
- Ending stickers, with their number of Scales
- Story cards and Dilemma outcomes, only when they change a rule or have a lasting effect

**Don't record secret information:**
- Agenda cards: they're personal secret objectives.
- Ideology cards drawn for a game.
- Anything not yet revealed, such as unopened Envelopes or Mystery boxes and the backs of Dilemma cards still in the deck.

## How to add content

Share photos of the revealed components in a Claude session on this repository, along with the game number, and ask Claude to record them. The steps Claude follows are in `CLAUDE.md`/`AGENTS.md` under "Recording revealed content".
