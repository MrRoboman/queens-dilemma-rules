# The Queen's Dilemma: Rules Digest

Summary of the **Core Rulebook v15** (`rulebook/QUD_Core_Rulebook_v15.pdf`). Citations look like **[p. N, Section]**. Page numbers are the ones printed on the rulebook pages, which match the PDF page numbers.

**Precedence:** When a card or sticker contradicts the rulebook, the component wins:
- Skill stickers **[p. 42]**
- Chronicle cards **[p. 45]**

**Spoilers:** Never read the back of a Dilemma card until its outcome is resolved **[p. 14, p. 21]**. Contents of the Legacy tray stay secret until the game tells you to open them. Ideology and Investment cards are public **[p. 5]**.

- **Resources** are only Gold, Food, Army and Materials. Power, points, tokens and markers are **not** Resources **[p. 15, "Resource or Not?"]**.
- **Ideology axes:** Tradition vs Progress, Force vs Diplomacy, Order vs Liberty **[p. 7]**.

## 1. Campaign Basics [p. 1, 6]
- This is a legacy campaign of about 14–18 games (30–40 hours). It cannot be reset unless you own the Recharge Pack **[p. 1]**.
- Best with 5–6 players and the same group throughout. **Players cannot be added to a campaign** **[p. 6, p. 21]**.
- No one wins a single game. Each game adds √, ∑ and † toward the campaign winner **[p. 6]**.

## 2. Before the First Game (one-time setup) [p. 8–14]
1. **Choose Characters** **[p. 8–10]**. Each Character's Ideologies:
   - Aesyas: Progress / Diplomacy
   - Elinor: Tradition / Order
   - Jilian: Diplomacy / Tradition
   - Lyrie: Liberty / Progress
   - Syd: Force / Liberty
   - Xanthe: Order / Force

   Each player takes their Character's Diary (write a House name on p. 1), Treasury box, 6 Heraldry tokens, 5 Ideology cards and Voting tile.
   - Recommended 3-player groups: Aesyas/Jilian/Syd, Aesyas/Elinor/Syd, Aesyas/Syd/Xanthe, Jilian/Lyrie/Xanthe.
   - Recommended 4-player groups: Aesyas/Jilian/Syd/Xanthe, Elinor/Jilian/Lyrie/Syd, Aesyas/Elinor/Lyrie/Xanthe **[p. 10]**.
2. **Starting Agenda** **[p. 11]**: Aesyas A106, Elinor A127, Jilian A044, Lyrie A064, Syd A086, Xanthe A017.
3. **Leader and Moderator** **[p. 11]**: Give the Leader token to a random player. Going clockwise, the other seats get these bonuses:
   - 2nd: 1∆
   - 3rd: 1∑
   - 4th: 1∆ + 1∑
   - 5th: 1∆ + 1º + 1∑
   - 6th: 1∆ + 1º + 1∑

   The **last** player in that order also gets the Moderator token. If the first game is played later, record this seating order.
4. **Regions** **[p. 12]**: There are 12 playable Regions. Aontas holds the capital and is not playable.
   - **Main Regions:** Starting with the Leader and going clockwise, each player picks 1 Region with a large Heraldry sticker. Then, starting with the Moderator and going counter-clockwise, each player picks a 2nd.
   - **Vassal Regions** (fewer than 6 players), marked with Vassal Heraldry stickers:
     - 5 players: the Moderator picks 1. The remaining Region goes to the player on the Moderator's right.
     - 4 players: starting with the Moderator and going counter-clockwise, each player picks 1.
     - 3 players: starting with the Leader and going clockwise, each player first picks a 3rd Main Region. Then, starting with the Moderator and going counter-clockwise, each player picks 1 Vassal Region.
   - Cover a Vassal Region's base Building type and Resources with a Resource Cover sticker. Vassal Regions produce nothing at first, but Buildings added there later do produce.
   - Tip: Main Regions whose base Buildings share a color give swingy income from game to game.
5. **Region Keyword stickers** go in the Keywords section of your Diary (from Diary p. 4). This includes Vassal Regions **[p. 13]**.
6. **Read the prologue** "War in Konfurtah" aloud **[p. 13]**.
7. **Starting Dilemma deck** **[p. 14]**: Open Envelope ˚00. Place its Story card on the matching Story slot. Shuffle its Dilemma cards and cover them with the Cover tile.
8. **Optional Age of Disorder content** **[p. 14]**. This can only be added now, never after the campaign starts:
   - DAOD: 4 Dilemma cards, shuffled into the deck.
   - CAOD: 4 Chronicle cards, which become the starting unlocked Chronicle deck in Save tray slot 3.
   - AAOD: 6 Agenda cards, one per Character (AAOD1 Aesyas, 2 Elinor, 3 Jilian, 4 Lyrie, 5 Syd, 6 Xanthe). Trash the cards of Characters not in play.

## 3. Setup Before Every Game [p. 15–21; cheatsheet p. 52]
1. **Map and supply** **[p. 15]**: Put the Ankist Map in the center with the Resources and ∆ in the general supply. Place **3∆ in Aontas**; this is the Power Pool.
2. **Timeline** **[p. 16]**: Mark the leftmost empty space. That space shows:
   - Its **color** (red or blue): which Buildings produce this game.
   - The **Bag number**: the Investment cost, also used by · on Agenda cards.
   - The **Resource icon**: which Resource is taxed at the end of the game.
3. **Ideology board** **[p. 16]**: Put each Slider in the middle of its track. Set Outcome markers and Hegemony tokens nearby.
4. **Legacy components** **[p. 17]**: Have the Legacy tray, Utility sheet and Building sheet ready.
5. **Player area** **[p. 17]**:
   - Pick seats; changing seats each game is suggested.
   - Take your items out of your Treasury and place your Voting tile Aye/Nay side up, with any Leader or Moderator token beside it.
   - Check your Skills and your Agenda goals.
6. **Ideology card** **[p. 18]**: Shuffle your 5 cards and draw 2. Keep the **higher-numbered** one secretly and return the rest. If you draw both cards marked "1", keep one of them.
7. **Income** **[p. 18]**: Take 7∆ (Starting Power), plus 1º, 1Ƒ, 1∞ and 1ª. Then collect the output of your Buildings that match this game's color. Base Buildings count as Buildings.
8. **Chronicle cards** **[p. 19]**:
   - Shuffle the unlocked Chronicle deck and draw up to 10 cards; the undrawn cards go back. Reveal the drawn cards one at a time:
     - **Instant** cards go to the light-blue slot, with no limit.
     - **Other** cards go to the light-brown slots, up to 5. If those are full, return the card unread.
   - Read the placed cards aloud and apply their **Herald effects**. For Instant cards, also apply the main effect.
   - Mark 1 Duration Track space on each card in a slot. A card whose track is now full plays its last game and is Trashed at cleanup.
9. **Investment draft** **[p. 20]**:
   - Shuffle the cards. Remove 1 at random with 5 players, or 2 with 3–4 players; removed cards stay unrevealed.
   - Players draft in order from **least to most √** (Diary p. 10). Ties go to the tied player closest to the Leader, clockwise.
   - Each player keeps 1 card and passes the rest. Unchosen cards go back unrevealed.
   - In the first game everyone has 0√, so the draft starts with the Leader and goes clockwise.
10. **Story cards** **[p. 20]**: Place them on the top-edge slots, sorted by symbol and number. You may reread them at any time.
11. **Event cards** **[p. 20]**: Place them on the bottom-right slots and apply their setup rules.
12. **Dilemma deck** **[p. 21]**: Shuffle it face up and cover it with the Cover tile. Never read card backs.

### Absences, Defections, Additions [p. 21]
- **Absent player:**
  - Use setup for the current player count.
  - The absent player gains or loses nothing (Resources, √, ∑, †) and cannot be targeted by special effects.
  - If they held the Leader or Moderator token, give it to a random player.
  - If one of their Keywords is called, resolve only its Legacy effects:
    - Attach their Heraldry sticker to Chronicle cards they unlock.
    - Attach Skill stickers to their Diary.
    - Put Agenda cards in their Treasury.
    - Attach Building stickers to one of their Regions, chosen by the Leader.
  - **On return:** they gain **3∑ for each game missed**, and must check overdue Agendas and resolve any Failure effects before the next game starts.
- **Defection:** Find a replacement if possible. Otherwise:
  - Use the setup and Mystery box rules for the new player count.
  - The defector's Keywords now **target the Moderator**.
- **Additions:** Not possible once the game has started.

## 4. Round Structure [p. 22]
A game lasts about 7–9 rounds, each built around one Dilemma card. Each round has 5 phases:
1. Read the Dilemma
2. Voting Phase
3. Vote Resolution
4. Dilemma Resolution
5. Check whether the end of the game is triggered

### 4.1 Read the Dilemma [p. 22]
- Draw from the **bottom** of the deck, face up. If the deck is empty, see the Troubleshooting Tool **[p. 49]**.
- Anyone may read the card aloud. Announce all highlighted **Keywords**.
- Declare the outcomes printed on the front: the Ideology shifts and the ≥/≤/ˆ Legacy hints.
- Hidden consequences are possible, so read the whole text **[p. 23, "Unexpected Consequences"]**.

### 4.2 Voting Phase [p. 23–26]
- Mark the possible outcomes on the Ideology board: **blue** markers for Aye and **red** for Nay. This includes the ≥/≤/ˆ spaces.
- Turns start with the **Leader** and go clockwise. Each turn you **Vote** or **Pass**. The phase can go around the table several times.
- **Voting actions** **[p. 23]**:
  - **Vote Aye/Nay:** Put 1 or more ∆ from your Treasury on the blue (Aye) or red (Nay) side of your tile.
  - **Raise:** If you have already voted, add 1 or more ∆ to the **same** side.
  - **Stay:** If you have already voted, add nothing. You may raise later.
  - Once you vote you **cannot pass** this phase, and **cannot switch sides** **[p. 26]**.
- **Taking the Leader token** **[p. 24]**: At the end of your voting turn, if you have the **strictly most** ∆ on your tile, whichever side it is on, take the Leader token immediately. Tying the top amount is not enough.
- **Pass** **[p. 24]**: Flip your tile to the Pass side and do **1 Pass Action** plus **1 Pass Bonus** if one is available. You cannot rejoin this Vote.
  - **Pass Actions:**
    - **Gather Power:** Put 1 Heraldry token on Aontas. You share the Power Pool at the end of the phase.
    - **Become Moderator:** Take the Moderator token, even from yourself, and put it **on your Voting tile**. Nobody else can take it this phase.
    - **Special Pass Action:** One granted by a Chronicle card, Skill or Event.
  - **Pass Bonus:** Unlocked by a Chronicle card, Event or Skill.
- **Using actions on Chronicle and Event cards** **[p. 25]**:
  - Put your Heraldry token in the Slot next to the card. Each action can be used by **one player per Voting Phase**.
  - You cannot use an action if:
    - its Slot is occupied;
    - the card has no empty Pass Action spaces (cards with no spaces at all are exempt);
    - you cannot pay its cost; or
    - a card rule forbids it.
- **Bargaining** **[p. 25]**:
  - Bargaining is allowed **only during the Voting Phase**. Resources (º, Ƒ, ∞, ª) may be given **for a service**: a vote, a pass, Branding rights, a tie-break, and so on.
  - **Not allowed:**
    - Trading Resources for Resources
    - Trading ∆
    - Trading ownership of the Leader or Moderator token
  - A Deal is **binding once the Resources are accepted**. Promises without an exchange are not binding and may be bluffs.
  - Write long-term deals in your Diary's Notes page.
- **End of the Voting Phase** **[p. 26]**: The phase ends after the turn of the player **to the right of the current Leader**. Because the Leader can change, the number of turns is not fixed.

### 4.3 Vote Resolution [p. 27–28]
- Add up the ∆ on each side, leaving it on the tiles. **The higher total wins.**
- **Tie:** The **Moderator** chooses the winning side.
- **Everyone passed:** The Moderator chooses the outcome **and takes the Leader token**, and counts as the Winner **[p. 41]**.
- **Leader on the losing side:** The Leader token goes to the winning-side player who put in the most ∆. Ties among them are broken by the Moderator.
- **Cleanup after the Vote** **[p. 28]**:
  1. **Power distribution:** Split the Power Pool evenly among the Gather Power players. Any remainder stays in the Pool.
  2. **Reset Moderator:** The Moderator token goes from the Voting tile back to the table in front of its holder. Become Moderator is available again next Voting Phase.
  3. **Reset special actions:**
     - Chronicle cards: move your Heraldry token from the Slot to the card's highest empty Pass Action space. When all spaces are full, the action is gone for the rest of the game. A card marked "any number" stacks tokens.
     - Event cards: follow the Event's rules.
     - Cards with no spaces: take your token back.
  4. **Power cleanup:** **Losers take their ∆ back** into their Treasury. **Winners' ∆ goes into the Power Pool**.

### 4.4 Dilemma Resolution [p. 29–31]
Do these steps in order:
- **a. Read the outcome:** Flip the card **horizontally if Aye** won or **vertically if Nay** won, then read it aloud.
- **b. Keyword effects:** Resolve one at a time, **left to right**. Targets can be Players, Winners, Leader or specific Keywords.
- **c. Ideology board:**
  - Move the Sliders as shown. Extra movement past the end of a track is wasted.
  - **Each Ideology that wins a Vote gets 1 Hegemony token**, even if its Slider did not move. Each token is worth 1√ to players scoring that Ideology.
- **d. Envelope (˚XX)** **[p. 30–31]**:
  1. Read the Story card aloud; it is always first in the Envelope. Show its art.
  2. Apply its effects.
  3. Return the empty Envelope to the Legacy tray.
  4. Put the Story card on top of its Storyline stack.
  5. Shuffle the 3 Dilemma cards into the deck.
  - A Story card with a gold symbol on black ends its Storyline. The Leader attaches the Ending sticker it names **[p. 47]**.
  - **Forgetting an Envelope can stall the campaign.**
- **e. Mystery box (˘MXX)** **[p. 31]**: Open it and immediately follow the WARNING! box at the top of its rules sheet. Add its Dilemma cards to the deck right away.
- **f. New round** **[p. 31]**:
  - Put the Dilemma card halfway under the lowest free Round Counter slot, back side up, so the outcome stays visible.
  - Remove all Outcome markers.
  - Draw the next Dilemma unless the end of the game was triggered.

### 4.5 Triggering the End of the Game [p. 32]
The game ends when **either** of these happens:
- A card with the **sand timer** on its back is placed on one of the **last 3** Round Counter slots.
- The **9th** Dilemma card is placed on the last slot.

Before ending, finish all of that card's effects: open its Envelopes and Mystery boxes and set them up fully.

If all 7 Ending stickers are attached, the game can also end when the Dilemma deck runs out. That game is the campaign's final session.

## 5. End of the Game [p. 33–37]
### 5.1 Event Phase [p. 33]
Resolve active Events in ascending card-number order. Skip this phase if there are none.

### 5.2 Scoring Phase [p. 33–35]
**a. Chronicle cards:** Check all active Chronicle cards. "End of Game" effects apply now.

**b. Ideology scoring** **[p. 33–34]**: Reveal your Ideology card.
- **Winning Ideology:** The Slider is on that Ideology's side of its track. A Slider at the exact center means neither side is winning.
- **Favored Ideology:**
  - Gain the card bonus (+3√ or +1√, as printed) if it is winning.
  - Gain 1√ per Hegemony token on that Ideology.
  - If it has **0 tokens**, gain **1∑ instead**.
- **Balanced pair:**
  - Gain **+2√** if the Slider is in one of the **3 silver middle spaces**.
  - Gain 1√ per token in the **smaller** of the two piles.
  - If the smaller pile has 0 tokens, gain **1∑ instead**.
- **Example 1:** Xanthe favors Order and Force. Order has 2 tokens and is not winning: 2√. Force has 2 tokens and is winning with +1: 3√. She scores 5√ in total **[p. 34]**.
- **Example 2:** Lyrie favors Liberty (+3) and balances Force/Diplomacy (+2).
  - Liberty has 3 tokens and is winning: 3√ + 3√.
  - Force has 1 token and Diplomacy has 0, so the smaller pile is empty: 1∑. The Slider is on a silver space: +2√.
  - She scores 8√ and 1∑ in total **[p. 34]**.

**c. Power scoring** **[p. 34]**: The most leftover ∆ in a Treasury gains **2∑**, and the second-most gains **1∑**. All tied players get the bonus for their place.

**d. Investments** **[p. 34–35]**:
- Pay the **Bag value** of the current Timeline space in the Resource shown on your card to get **Success**.
- If you cannot or choose not to pay, you get **Failure**.
- Paying is voluntary, so you cannot pay with Infamy instead **[p. 40]**.

**e. Agenda resolution** **[p. 35, 46]**:
- Pay the costs to complete Agendas and get **Success**, then Trash the card.
- Then any Agenda whose **time limit equals the current game number** fails. Apply its Failure box and Trash it.
- Agendas drawn during this phase may be completed right away. **Crossroad** Agendas must be resolved immediately.
- Many results tell you to draw a new Agenda; forgetting to do so can stall the campaign.

**f. Taxes** **[p. 35]**:
- Discard **half (rounded up)** of the Resource taxed by the current Timeline space.
- If you have none of it, you pay 0.
- You **cannot take Infamy instead**.

### 5.3 Cleanup [p. 36]
- Trash the resolved Dilemma cards from the Round Counter.
- Trash depleted Chronicle cards, meaning those with a full Duration Track. All other Chronicle cards are shuffled back into the unlocked Chronicle deck.
- **Discard all leftover ∆** to the supply. Power never carries over between games.

### 5.4 Save the Game [p. 37–38]
- Resources in your Treasury **carry over**. Put your Diary, Agendas, Ideology cards, Voting tile, Heraldry tokens and any Leader or Moderator token in your Treasury.
- **Save tray:**
  1. Dilemma deck with the Cover tile.
  2. Story cards.
  3. Unlocked Chronicle deck, Event cards and Investment cards.
  4. A large bag of Resources and ∆, and a small bag of Sliders, Outcome markers and Hegemony tokens.

## 6. End of the Campaign [p. 39]
- At the end of each game, check two things: are all **7 Ending stickers** attached, and do **3 or fewer** Dilemma cards remain? If both are true, Trash the remaining Dilemmas.
- The **next** game is the **Grand Finale**: open Mystery boxes **M07 and M08**, which decide the campaign winner.
- **Final score inputs:** √ and ∑, plus your Infamy penalty tier.
  - √ is worth more if the Kingdom is **stable**; ∑ is worth more if it is **unstable**.
  - Stability equals the number of **Scale** icons on the Ending stickers **[p. 39, 47]**:
    - 0 Scales: negative for stability.
    - 1 Scale: mixed.
    - 2 Scales: broadly positive.

## 7. Points, Power, Resources [p. 39–40]
- **√, ∑ and †** are marked in your Diary tracks.
- **Infamy penalty tiers:** Each 5† unlocks a tier. Only the **last fully unlocked tier** counts against you at the end of the campaign **[p. 39]**.
- **Gaining and losing ∆:**
  - Gained ∆ comes from the supply.
  - **Lost ∆ goes to the Power Pool**, so ∆ never leaves play unless an effect says so **[p. 40]**.
- **Gaining and losing Resources:** Gained Resources come from the supply. Lost or spent Resources go back to the supply.
- **Gold conversion:** Whenever you spend Resources, **2º can replace any 1 other Resource** **[p. 40]**.
- **Forced loss vs Infamy** **[p. 40]**:
  - If you are **forced** to lose Resources, you may pay any amount and take **+1† per Resource you don't pay**, either by choice or because you can't.
  - Example: you must pay 3∞ but have 1∞. Pay the 1 and take 2†, or pay nothing and take 3†.
  - For **voluntary** costs you **cannot** use Infamy. If you can't pay, you can't take the action. Voluntary costs include:
    - Investments
    - Agendas
    - Pass Actions and Pass Bonuses
    - Skill activations
  - Taxes cannot be swapped for Infamy either **[p. 35]**.

## 8. Keywords and Effects [p. 41]
- **Keyword targets:**
  - **Winners:** everyone who won the last Vote. If everyone passed, the Moderator is the Winner.
  - **Leader** and **Moderator:** whoever currently holds that token.
  - **Players:** everyone, including players who lost or passed.
  - **Character Name:** that Character's owner.
  - **Region:** that Region's owner, Vassal Regions included **[p. 13]**.
  - **Narrative Keywords:** whoever has that sticker.
- An effect with no target applies to the player performing the action. **Agenda effects target the card's owner** unless stated otherwise.
- **Multiple effects** resolve in order: **left to right, then top to bottom**.
- **Effect codes:**
  - ±Resources and ±∆
  - +√, +∑ and +†
  - ˚XX: Envelope
  - ˘MXX: Mystery box
  - ¯AXXX: Agenda card
  - ˇBXX: Building sticker
  - ∏CXXX: Chronicle card
  - ¬KXX: Keyword sticker
  - ΩSXX: Skill sticker
  - ENDXX: Ending sticker

## 9. Legacy Elements [p. 42–47]
- **Skill stickers (ΩS)** **[p. 42]**:
  - The target reads the sticker aloud and attaches it to the first free Skills space (Diary p. 6 onward).
  - If no space is free, cover an old sticker; inactive ones are suggested first.
  - To activate a Skill, read it aloud and resolve it.
  - If the sticker has an Activation Track, mark one space per use. A full track makes the Skill inactive. Without a track, uses are unlimited unless the Skill says otherwise.
  - **The Skill overrides the rulebook.**
- **Building stickers (ˇB)** **[p. 43]**:
  - Attach in any of your Regions, Main or Vassal.
  - If the target was a Region Keyword, attach in **that** Region. If a black box names a location, attach it there.
  - There is no limit per Region. Don't cover villages, rivers, roads, their names or the fold lines.
  - Buildings produce only in games of their **banner color**. For an "A/B" Building, choose one Resource each time it produces.
  - Base Buildings count as Buildings for all purposes.
- **Keyword stickers (¬K)** **[p. 44]**: Read aloud and attach to the first free Keywords space (Diary p. 4 onward). Your Keywords are public on request.
- **Chronicle cards (∏C)** **[p. 44–45]**:
  - **Brand** the card with your small Heraldry sticker to become its **Herald**. Cards with a pre-printed Heraldry don't need Branding; cards without a Herald space have no Herald.
  - A newly unlocked card has **no effect this game**. Shuffle it into the unlocked deck immediately.
  - **Band color:**
    - Light blue: Setup.
    - Yellow: during play.
    - Dark blue: end of the game.
  - **Band labels:** Instant, Pass Action, Pass Bonus, Voting Phase, End of the Game, Ideology Scoring, Power Scoring, Investment Phase, Tax Phase.
  - **Duration Track:** The maximum number of games the card can be in play. Cards with no track never expire, and cards from a Storyline ending (black END box) have no limit.
  - If you run out of small Heraldry stickers, write the Character's initial instead.
  - **The Chronicle card overrides the rulebook.**
- **Agenda cards (¯A)** **[p. 46–47]**:
  - When you unlock one, read its back secretly. If the time-limit box is blank, **write the current game number + 3**.
  - The goal is usually to spend Resources. "·" means the current Timeline Bag value.
  - The time limit is the last game in which you can complete the goal; after it, apply Failure.
  - **Crossroad:** A choice between two options, resolved immediately.
  - **Epilogue:** Its final reward is gained immediately. Mark a Fate space (Diary p. 3), then draw an Ongoing Agenda.
  - **Ongoing (¯∞):** No time limit and no Failure. When completed, it isn't Trashed; return it to the Legacy tray's Ongoing pile and draw a random new one.
  - The final objective of a Character Storyline has gold rewards.
  - If you draw a Storyline Agenda in another Character's colors, it's a mistake. Return it and check the code.
- **Ending stickers (END)** **[p. 47]**:
  - The **Leader** attaches the sticker over that Storyline's slot.
  - From then on, keep that Storyline's Story cards in the Save tray instead of on the map.

## 10. Chronicle Symbol Triggers and Targets [p. 49]
- **Triggers:**
  - **WIN:** the Ideology is winning.
  - **MAX:** the Slider is at the far end of that Ideology's side.
  - **LOSE:** the Slider is on the opposing side.
- **Targets:**
  - **Players:** everyone.
  - **Scoring for an Ideology:** **Favored** Ideologies only, not Balanced.
  - **Majority (>) / Minority (<)** of a Resource, point type or ∆: **all tied players are targeted**.
  - **N+ Buildings of a type:** players with at least that many.
  - **Character icon:** the normal Keyword rules apply.

## 11. Quick Rulings and Common Disputes
| Question | Ruling | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| Does tying the top bid make you Leader? | No, you need strictly the most ∆ on your tile. | p. 24 |
| Can I switch from Aye to Nay? | No, you can only Raise or Stay. | p. 26 |
